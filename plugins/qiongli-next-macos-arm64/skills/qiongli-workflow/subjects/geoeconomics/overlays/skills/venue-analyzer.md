## Geoeconomics Overlay

### Activation
Apply this overlay when `venue-analyzer` is used for geoeconomics manuscripts targeting international security, IPE, political economy, global policy, or related interdisciplinary venues.

### Required Context
- Require target venue, paper type, instrument, sender, target, response channel, evidence type, and strategic objective.
- Require whether the manuscript speaks mainly to international security, IPE, economics, finance, trade, or policy audiences.
- Require the current balance between scholarly contribution and policy relevance.

### Subject-Specific Procedure
1. Classify venue fit by contribution type: security strategy, IPE mechanism, political economy design, market evidence, or policy synthesis.
2. Check whether the paper has enough economic instrument evidence for geoeconomics rather than general geopolitical commentary.
3. Match claim strength to venue expectations for causal design, case evidence, archival evidence, network measurement, or policy relevance.
4. Identify the most likely desk-reject reason for each candidate venue.
5. Recommend venue positioning language that separates strategic claim, economic evidence, and policy implication.

### Reviewer-Risk Checks
- Check whether international-security venues would see too little security contribution.
- Check whether IPE or economics venues would see too little mechanism or identification evidence.
- Check whether policy venues would require clearer implications and less overclaiming.

### Output Requirements
- The venue analysis must include `Venue Family`, `Primary Audience`, `Evidence Standard`, `Desk-Reject Risk`, and `Positioning Revision`.
- Each venue recommendation must state which geoeconomic evidence is strong and which claim must be narrowed.
- Missing venue-specific standards must be recorded as a profile gap.

### Blocked Conditions
- Block venue recommendations when the manuscript's evidence type is unknown.
- Block strategic competition positioning when the target venue would require direct security or statecraft evidence.
- Do not recommend a top venue based only on topical salience.
