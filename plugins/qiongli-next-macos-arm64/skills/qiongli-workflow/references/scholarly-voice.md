# Scholarly Voice: English and Chinese

Use with J2 for stiff, translated or generic scholarly prose. Resolve the
specific expression problem; sound academic by being precise, not ornate.

## Shared editing decisions

- Keep names, numbers, quotations, equations, citations, terms, negation,
  quantifiers, scope and uncertainty. Suspected factual errors need a verification
  note; editing is not permission to correct the underlying research silently.
- Follow a supplied voice sample or venue rule without inventing personality,
  experience or a stronger position. Preserve required AI disclosure.
- Locate the point of each paragraph and the role of each sentence. Keep a
  stable topic and identifiable referents; use a paragraph break for a topic
  change when a supported transition cannot connect it naturally.
- Distinguish a genuine contrast from simple addition, and an observed sequence
  from causation. Retain helpful transitions; do not manufacture a missing premise
  with “therefore” or “因此”. Flag the missing reasoning instead.
- Edit at the requested depth. A language pass preserves section order and
  fixed headings; structural editing needs a request that covers it. For coaching,
  show a few meaningful revisions and explain the decisions the author must make.
- Stop when the text meets the request and the source comparison passes. No
  detector target, banned-word list, required sentence pattern or forced slang.

## English output

Use established collocations and keep the main subject and verb easy to follow.
Reduce noun chains and stacked modifiers without dropping qualifications. Keep
technical terms consistent instead of cycling through near-synonyms. Active or
passive voice and first person follow meaning and venue, not universal rules.
Preserve statistical “significant” and exact Methods procedures.

Carry a recognizable topic into the next sentence, then introduce the new point.
Clarify “this”, “it” or “they” only when the source establishes the referent.
Use sentence length to fit the idea. When translating, reconstruct English
clause order and articles rather than carrying over the source grammar.

Example (expression only):

> We conducted an examination of the association in 120 firms. The estimate was
> 0.18 (p = .03), but the design does not establish causality [12].

Can become:

> We examined the association in 120 firms. The estimate was 0.18 (p = .03), but
> the design does not establish causality [12].

The numbers, citation and causal limitation stay exact. “The effect proves…”
would change the research claim, regardless of how fluent it sounds.

## 中文输出

按读者、学科和用途确定语体；简繁体随原稿或用户要求。普通学术说明应清楚平实，
正式论文保持学科所需的严谨程度；不把长句、抽象名词或公文化表达当作严谨。

先理顺段落意思，再调整信息顺序。能用具体动词时，减少“对……进行……”等空架子；
拆开堆叠的修饰语和“的”字链。主语可省略，但换话题时要明确指代。不要为了避免
重复而更换关键术语，也不要把所有长句切成短句。

“因此”须有原文支持的推论，“然而”须有真实转折。“此外”不能弥补话题脱节。
保留必要的连接词，不强制排比、四字短语或段末总结。处理中译英、英译中时，
按目标语言重组语序，不新增理由、例子或结论。

例如：

> 本研究对120家企业中的关联进行了检验，估计值为0.18（p = .03）。然而，
> 该研究设计不能确立因果关系[12]。

可改为：

> 本研究检验了120家企业中的关联，估计值为0.18（p = .03），但该研究设计不能
> 确立因果关系[12]。

“相关”不可润色成“导致”，“尚不能排除”不可简化成“没有”。完成后连贯通读，
再对照原稿核查研究含义、数据、引文和限制条件。
