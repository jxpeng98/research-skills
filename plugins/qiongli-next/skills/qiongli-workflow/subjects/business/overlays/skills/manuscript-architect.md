## Business Overlay

### Activation
Apply this overlay when `manuscript-architect` is used for business, management, strategy, organization, marketing, operations, or entrepreneurship manuscripts.

### Required Context
- Require the target literature stream, business phenomenon, theory contribution, core constructs, level of analysis, empirical setting, method, and target journal family.
- Require whether the manuscript is theory-building, theory-testing, qualitative, quantitative, mixed-method, review, or methods-oriented.
- Require the strongest rival framing that could reduce the paper to a descriptive case, consulting report, or class project.

### Subject-Specific Procedure
1. Architect the introduction around a doctoral-level journal contribution to a specific business theory conversation.
2. Separate phenomenon, theory mechanism, empirical setting, managerial implication, and boundary conditions.
3. For qualitative work, plan a transparent evidence chain from sampling to coding to constructs, including negative cases.
4. For quantitative work, connect hypotheses to construct validity, level of analysis, sample construction, model fit, and robustness evidence.
5. Place rival explanations and practical implications after theory contribution, not as substitutes for it.

### Reviewer-Risk Checks
- Check whether the manuscript has setting novelty but no theory contribution.
- Check whether constructs drift across introduction, theory, methods, and findings.
- Check whether the level of analysis in hypotheses, data, model, and claims is inconsistent.

### Output Requirements
- The manuscript architecture must include `Target Conversation`, `Theory Contribution`, `Construct Map`, `Evidence Chain`, and `Reviewer Rejection Risk`.
- Every central claim must state construct, level of analysis, evidence source, and contribution risk.
- Missing theory conversation, construct validity, or method transparency must be converted into revision tasks.

### Blocked Conditions
- Block submission-ready architecture when the contribution is only managerial implication or descriptive setting novelty.
- Block hypothesis architecture when constructs or levels of analysis are undefined.
- Do not present undergraduate-and-above work as journal-ready until it is raised to doctoral-level journal standards.
