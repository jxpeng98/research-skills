## Geoeconomics Overlay

### Activation
Apply this overlay when `manuscript-architect` is used for geoeconomics manuscripts on economic statecraft, sanctions, export controls, supply chains, financial tools, industrial policy, or strategic competition.

### Required Context
- Require the sender, target, instrument, intermediary, affected market, response channel, strategic objective, evidence source, and target venue family.
- Require whether the strategic economic statecraft claim concerns coercion, deterrence, resilience, signaling, industrial policy, strategic substitution, or mixed objectives.
- Require exposure construction and timing before drafting empirical or policy claims.

### Subject-Specific Procedure
1. Architect the introduction around the economic statecraft claim before broad geopolitical context.
2. Define sender, target, instrument, intermediaries, market response, and strategic competition logic.
3. Separate national-security rhetoric from direct evidence about sanctions, supply chain exposure, financial statecraft, or industrial policy.
4. Connect each central claim to exposure, timing, target response, substitution, evasion, or countermeasure evidence.
5. Calibrate policy implications when strategic competition evidence is indirect or incomplete.

### Reviewer-Risk Checks
- Check whether the manuscript describes a policy instrument without sender-target-response logic.
- Check whether supply chain or network exposure is asserted without measurement.
- Check whether strategic claims exceed market, firm, trade, financial, or policy evidence.

### Output Requirements
- The manuscript architecture must include `Instrument-Sender-Target Map`, `Exposure Logic`, `Response Channel`, `Strategic Objective`, and `Policy Claim Calibration`.
- Each central claim must separate economic evidence from security rhetoric.
- Missing exposure, timing, or response evidence must become a revision task.

### Blocked Conditions
- Block strategic competition claims when the evidence only describes policy announcements.
- Block supply chain or sanctions claims when exposure construction is absent.
- Do not present policy relevance as proof of a geoeconomic mechanism.
